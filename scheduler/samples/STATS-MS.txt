Program Overview
The Master of Science in Statistics and the Master of Science in Statistics (Data Science track) are terminal degree programs designed to prepare students for careers in statistics, data science, and related fields. These programs are not pathways to the PhD in Statistics; students interested in doctoral study should apply directly to the PhD program.

Breadth Requirement
Breadth courses are meant to  complement their statistical training with graduate-level courses from a variety of disciplines. Courses develop relevant skills or emphasize applications of statistics and probability and expand their interests in areas such as optimization, scientific computing, economics, quantitative finance, healthcare, operations research, and environmental science.
