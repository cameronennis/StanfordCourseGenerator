Prerequisite Calculus Course

The minimum prerequisite for a Civil Engineering Minor is MATH19 Calculus (or MATH20 Calculus or MATH21 Calculus).

Complete at least 1 of the following Courses:
MATH19 - Calculus

MATH20 - Calculus

MATH21 - Calculus

Requirements Guidelines

Since undergraduates with varying backgrounds may be interested in obtaining a civil engineering minor, and the field itself is so broad, no single set of course requirements will be appropriate for all students. Instead, interested students are encouraged to propose courses within the guidelines below.

A civil engineering minor must contain at least 24 units of coursework not taken for the major and consist of at least six classes of at least three units each of letter-graded work.
