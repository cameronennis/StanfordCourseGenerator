Program Overview

The mission of the minor in Mathematics is to provide students with the core knowledge to analyze and solve multivariable problems and to acquire experience with logical reasoning and generalization.

Minor Requirements

The Math minor consists of six math courses at or above Math 51.

Minors must complete at least three 100-level and above courses within the six math courses

STATS116 (No longer offered), STATS118 or STATS200 (at most one, but not both and neither if STATS116 is being used), PHIL151, PHIL152 count as 100-level math courses for the Math minor requirement. No other courses outside the Math Department can count in this way. There are no exceptions to this for any reason.

MATH190, MATH193, MATH193X, course, MATH198, and MATH199 cannot count toward the minor

All 100-level and above courses must be taken at Stanford.

Only Math 50/60CM/60DM series and first-year calculus can be double counted toward any other major or minor

Recommended (NOT REQUIRED) Courses

It is recommended (but not required) that the six courses towards the minor include one multivariable mathematics series:

MATH51, MATH52, MATH53.

MATH61CM, MATH62CM, MATH63CM.

MATH61DM, MATH62DM, MATH63DM.
