Program Overview
The minor in Spanish is for students who want to combine the acquisition of linguistic competence with the study of the literatures and cultures of the Spanish-speaking world.
Declaring the Minor
To declare, students should complete the
Spanish declaration worksheet
, sending it to the department Director of Undergraduate Studies (DUS) for approval. Once signed, they should send the worksheet to the
DLCL Undergraduate Student Services Officer
via email, before completing the final step of declaration via Axess.
Requirements Overview
To earn a minor in Spanish, students' plans must adhere to these common overarching requirements:
Complete at least six courses at three units or more and 23 overall units of coursework in the department.
These 23 units may not be used toward any other major or minor.
Courses applied to the minor must be taken for a letter grade.

Electives
Language coursework.
Up to 12 second-year or above Spanish language units may count towards the degree, not including conversational, oral communication, business, or medical language courses.
Independent Study.
With the approval of the Director of Undergraduate Studies, Independent Study courses may count towards the degree.
Bing Overseas Program.
Up to 10 units of pre-approved BOSP coursework may contribute to the minor.
Coursework outside the department.
One approved 3-5 unit course (5 units maximum) from outside ILAC or the Language Center.
