Program Overview
A minor in Human Biology introduces students to the interdisciplinary study of human beings from biological, behavioral, social, and cultural perspectives. Many serious problems facing humans today involve biological and social aspects. Scientific approaches to these problems are essential but must be broadly conceived and thoughtfully implemented with regard to the social and cultural contexts. Students with a minor in Human Biology are expected to develop a strong content background and the skills to integrate human beings’ biological and social aspects.
Students declaring a minor in Human Biology must submit a
Minor Plan Form
at minimum two quarters before their intended quarter of degree conferral (for example, a student must declare a minor before the end of autumn quarter if graduating in the following spring quarter). Upon approval from student services, students can declare the HumBio minor in Axess.

Completion requirement
Individualized Plan Elective Courses (3 courses; 10 units required)
To fulfill the requirement between HumBio Elective course and Depth Elective courses, you must complete 3 courses, totaling a minimum of 10 units. These courses should be upper-level, non-HumBio Core or equivalent courses, related to an area of emphasis within the Human Biology curriculum. Elective courses for an individualized plan must be pre-approved by the HumBio advising team.
This option is for students who do not declare an official subplan.
