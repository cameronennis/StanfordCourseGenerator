Program Overview
The minor program in Linguistics aims to provide students with basic knowledge in the principal areas of linguistics (phonetics, phonology, morphology, syntax, semantics, pragmatics, historical linguistics, sociolinguistics, psycholinguistics, and computational linguistics). Requirements for the minor include at least 28 units of coursework in Linguistics and related fields, approved in advance by the Linguistics Director of Undergraduate Studies.

Electives
The remaining courses toward the total 28 units are determined in advance consultation with the Linguistics Director of Undergraduate Studies. Students are encouraged to take at least one 200-level Linguistics course. Students may also choose to do independent work with a faculty member of their choice.
