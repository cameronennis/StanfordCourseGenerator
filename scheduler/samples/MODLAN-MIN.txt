Program Overview
The Division of Literatures, Cultures, and Languages offers an undergraduate minor in Modern Languages that permits students to demonstrate strength in two distinct modern languages and their literatures. The minor draws upon courses housed within the DLCL, East Asian Languages and Cultures, the Stanford Language Center, and the Special Language Program.
Declaring the Minor
To declare, students should complete the
Modern Languages declaration worksheet
, sending it to the department Faculty Advisor for approval. Once signed, they should send the worksheet to the
Undergraduate Student Services Officer (DLCL)
via email, before completing the final step of declaration via Axess.
Requirements Overview
Students’ plans must adhere to these common overarching requirements:
Students will study two modern languages, Language A and Language B.
Complete at least six courses at three units or more and a total of 22 units
Coursework may not duplicate work counted toward other majors or minors
Courses applied to the minor must be taken for a letter grade

Language "A" Requirements
Fulfill ALL of the following requirements:
Language Courses
Students must complete TWO language courses at the second-year level or beyond, for a minimum of eight units total.
Note: Language courses may not include conversational, oral communication, business, or medical language courses.
AND
Literature Course
Students must complete ONE literature course for at least 3 units.
Literature courses from BOSP programs are pre-approved for the minor. Literature courses taught by Stanford faculty will be approved with the Faculty Advisor's permission.
