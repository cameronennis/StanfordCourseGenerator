Program Overview
An understanding of chemistry enables students to participate in research and studies including biotechnology, nanotechnology, catalysis, human health, materials science, earth and environmental sciences, and more. Complement your undergraduate major with an overview of the subdisciplines of chemistry — including organic, inorganic, physical, biological, theoretical, and analytical — that support careers in various fields.
