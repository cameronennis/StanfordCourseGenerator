Program Overview
To declare a minor in Anthropology, contact the Undergraduate Student Services Officer (SSO) to discuss the requirements and prepare the Minor Form found on the
department website
. After meeting with the SSO, apply in Axess for the minor in Anthropology. Request a faculty advisor assignment from the SSO. Once confirmed, meet with the assigned faculty advisor to receive approval of the minor form. Submit completed form to SSO.

Completion requirement
Department Emphasis Coursework
Students should complete a minimum of 10 units from the courses listed below. Departmental courses may fulfill the requirements for more than one emphasis. You will work with your faculty advisor to select emphasis courses related to a plan of study with a specific focus on a theoretical and/or geographical area.
Fulfill ANY of the following requirements:
Cultural & Social Anthropology Emphasis
Departmental courses outside of this list may be counted toward emphasis but will need faculty advisor approval. For non-departmental courses, faculty advisor approval is needed along with a petition to the Undergraduate Committee via the Undergraduate Exception Form found on the
department website
.
Complete ANY of the following Courses:
ANTHRO1 - Introduction to Cultural and Social Anthropology
ANTHRO39 - Sense of Place
ANTHRO126 - The Urban Century: The City in Global Perspective
ANTHRO132 - Anthropology of Islam
ANTHRO133 - Masculinity: Technologies and Cultures of Gender in the Age of AI
ANTHRO157 - Japanese Anthropology
OR
Environmental Anthropology Emphasis
Departmental courses outside of this list may be counted toward emphasis but will need faculty advisor approval. For non-departmental courses, faculty advisor approval is needed along with a petition to the Undergraduate Committee via the Undergraduate Exception Form found on the
department website
.
Complete ANY of the following Courses:
ANTHRO103 - The Archaeology of Climate
ANTHRO167 - Body and Environment
ANTHRO198A - Archaeological Geographic Information Systems
OR
Medical Anthropology Emphasis
Departmental courses outside of this list may be counted toward emphasis but will need faculty advisor approval. For non-departmental courses, faculty advisor approval is needed along with a petition to the Undergraduate Committee via the undergraduate Exception Form found on the department website.
Complete ANY of the following Courses:
ANTHRO82 - Medical Anthropology
ANTHRO151 - Medical Anthropology, a Global View
ANTHRO153 - Asylum: Knowledge, Politics, and Population
ANTHRO186 - Culture and Madness: Anthropological and Psychiatric Approaches to Mental Illness
OR
Self-Designed Emphasis
Undergraduates may also request an exception from the undergraduate committee for a self-designed emphasis in the Anthropology minor by submitting the Undergraduate Course Exception Form found on the
department website
.
Anthropology Elective Coursework
The remainder of the 30 required units may be fulfilled with any Anthropology courses, or a combination of Anthropology courses and up to successfully petitioned 10 units from related areas of study, overseas studies, and/or transfer units.
No more than 5 units of directed reading-style/ research coursework listed may be counted toward the minor.
