Program Overview
The undergraduate minor in Statistics is designed to complement major degree programs primarily in the social and natural sciences. Students with an undergraduate Statistics minor gain theoretical and practical training in statistical methods. The minor:
equips students with foundational knowledge in probability and statistics
teaches students to correctly choose and apply statistical methods
Program Learning Outcomes
Students are expected to be able to:
Select the appropriate statistical methodology for the problem at hand
Analyze statistical methodology regarding its appropriateness and limitations critically
Present and interpret the result of statistical analyses

Program Requirements
Completion requirement
Qualifying Courses
Complete ANY of the following Courses:
ENGR108 - Introduction to Matrix Methods
MATH52 - Integral Calculus of Several Variables
MATH62CM - Modern Mathematics: Continuous Methods
MATH63DM - Modern Mathematics: Discrete Methods
MATH104 - Applied Matrix Theory
Note that CME 102 is not a suitable course to use in place of MATH 52; if a student has taken CME 102, they need to take one of the alternative courses to satisfy the Qualifying Course requirement.
MATH 52 can be double-counted between the statistics minor and a major or other minor.
Theoretical Statistics Requirement
Complete ANY of the following Courses:
STATS118 - Probability Theory for Statistical Inference
STATS200 - Introduction to Theoretical Statistics
OR
STATS200Q - Philosophical Foundations of Statistics
Students who take STATS 118 for this Theoretical Statistics Requirement can count STATS 200 or STATS 200Q toward the Elective Course requirement below.
If a student has taken MATH 151 or STATS 116X (as an Elective Course for the statistics minor or for any other program), they are required to take STATS 200 or STATS 200Q to fulfill the Theoretical Statistics Requirement.
STATS 118 has a prerequisite of STATS 117, which can count as an Elective Course below. CS 109, EE 178, and MS&E 120 also satisfy the prerequisite for STATS 118, but these classes may NOT count toward the Elective Courses requirement for the minor.
Applied Statistics Requirement
Complete ANY of the following Courses:
STATS191 - Introduction to Applied Statistics
STATS203 - Regression Models and Analysis of Variance
Elective Courses
