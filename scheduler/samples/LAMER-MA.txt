Program Overview
The Master of Arts in Latin American Studies is an interdisciplinary program designed to develop students' knowledge and skills in the study of Latin America and to prepare them for professional careers or doctoral study.
The curriculum comprises courses surveying the region, including its history, politics, society, ecology, and culture; advanced language training; and focused coursework within the program's three fields of specialization (humanities, environment and health, and social sciences). In consultation with their faculty advisor(s), students select a course of study suited to their interests. Students with little prior coursework in Latin American Studies may benefit most from this interdisciplinary approach.
In addition, students receive foundational training in systems and tools—such as data visualization, big data analysis, and geographic information systems (GIS)—to remain current and effective in their chosen careers.
Graduates of the program have entered a range of fields, including higher education, business, the private sector, government, and nonprofit organizations.

Elective courses overview
Students must take 5 units of elective courses from across the University's offerings, selected with guidance and approval from their faculty advisor(s) to further inform their academic and professional interests. A maximum of 5 elective units may be applied toward this requirement. Activity courses may not be used to satisfy this requirement.

Language Requirement
Completion requirement
Language requirement overview
Students must complete a three-course sequence in ONE relevant language of Latin America, totaling 9–14 units. This may include Spanish, Portuguese, Quechua, Nahuatl, Haitian Creole, or others, if viable.
The Stanford Language Center administers Spanish placement exams upon matriculation.
If incoming students do not meet the required entry level in Spanish—equivalent to B1 (CEFR scale), Intermediate High (ACTFL scale), or third-year university study—they must complete the sequence in this language.
