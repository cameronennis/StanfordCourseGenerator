Degree Requirements Overview

Each Physics major takes a set of required courses common to all pathways (Core Program Requirements and WIM), and an additional six courses in one of eight defined pathways or, in rare cases, an individually designed pathway (Depth in Discipline).

Introductory Physics Courses (23 units)
Complete ALL of the following Courses:
PHYSICS41 - Mechanics
OR PHYSICS41E - Mechanics, Concepts, Calculations, and Context

PHYSICS43 - Electricity and Magnetism

PHYSICS61 - Mechanics and Special Relativity

PHYSICS61L - Mechanics Laboratory

PHYSICS71 - Quantum and Thermal Physics

PHYSICS79L - Introduction to Data Analysis, with Python and Jupyter
OR PHYSICS89L - Introduction to Data Analysis, with Python and Jupyter

PHYSICS81 - Electricity and Magnetism Using Special Relativity and Vector Calculus

PHYSICS81L - Introduction to Experimental Practice
OR PHYSICS71L - Modern Physics Laboratory

Note that some students may be able to place out of Physics 41 and/or 43 based on advice from the Physics Placement Diagnostic.

Capstone Options

Students must complete ONE of the following three options to fulfill the Capstone requirement for the Physics major.

Fulfill ANY of the following requirements:
1. Complete a Senior Thesis

Earn at least 3 credits from the following:
PHYSICS205 - Senior Thesis Research

OR
2. Complete a Research Project Plus a Capstone Paper

Complete ANY of the following Courses:
PHYSICS191 - Scientific Communication in Physics

PHYSICS192 - Physics Capstone Paper

OR
3. Course Option
Complete at least 1 of the following Courses:
PHYSICS100 - Introduction to Observational Astrophysics

PHYSICS108 - Advanced Physics Laboratory: Project

PHYSICS171 - Thermodynamics, Kinetic Theory, and Statistical Mechanics II
