Program Overview

There are three options for students interested in a minor in Mechanical Engineering: A general minor that exposes students to the breadth of the field and two specialized minors -- Thermosciences and Mechanical Design -- that allow students to pursue a particular area in more depth.

Students interested in adding the minor should submit a minor program sheet to Mechanical Engineering Student Services.

Program Requirements
Completion requirement
Mechanical Engineering Minors
Fulfill ANY of the following requirements:
General Minor in Mechanical Engineering

Students interested in this minor must take the following courses as prerequisites: MATH19/MATH20/MATH21 or equivalent and PHYSICS41.

Fulfill ALL of the following requirements:
Required Courses
Complete ALL of the following Courses:
ENGR14 - Intro to Solid Mechanics

ENGR15 - Dynamics

ME30 - Engineering Thermodynamics

ME70 - Introductory Fluids Engineering

ME102 - Foundations of Product Realization

AND
Elective Courses
Complete at least 2 of the following Courses:
ME80 - Mechanics of Materials

ME103 - Product Realization: Design and Making

ME131 - Heat Transfer

ME161 - Dynamic Systems, Vibrations and Control

OR
Thermosciences Minor in Mechanical Engineering

Students interested in this minor must take the following courses as prerequisites: MATH19/MATH20/MATH21 or equivalent, MATH51 (or CME100), and PHYSICS41.

Complete ALL of the following Courses:
ENGR14 - Intro to Solid Mechanics

ME30 - Engineering Thermodynamics

ME70 - Introductory Fluids Engineering

ME131 - Heat Transfer

ME132 - Intermediate Thermodynamics

ME133 - Intermediate Fluid Mechanics

ME149 - Mechanical Measurements

OR
Mechanical Design Minor in Mechanical Engineering

This minor includes design activities supported by analysis and has the following courses as prerequisites: MATH19/MATH20/MATH21 or equivalent, and PHYSICS41.

Due to the number of overlapping core courses, Design (BS) majors may not add the Mechanical Design Minor in Mechanical Engineering.

Fulfill ALL of the following requirements:
Required Courses
Complete ALL of the following Courses:
ENGR14 - Intro to Solid Mechanics

ME80 - Mechanics of Materials

ME102 - Foundations of Product Realization

ME103 - Product Realization: Design and Making

ME104 - Mechanical Systems Design

AND
Elective Courses
Complete at least 2 of the following Courses:
ME127 - Design for Additive Manufacturing

ME128 - Computer-Aided Product Realization

ME129 - Manufacturing Processes and Design

ME210 - Introduction to Mechatronics

ME220 - Introduction to Sensors
