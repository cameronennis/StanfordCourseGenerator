Program Overview
Master’s in Translational Medicine (M-TRAM) is a professional degree program that trains students to advance promising biomedical solutions from early-stage research to full regulatory approval. Technology focus areas include immunotherapy, gene therapy, vaccines, biomarkers, single-cell omics, and medical devices. M-TRAM combines flexible classroom learning with hands-on, team-based medical research, one-on-one mentoring by renowned Stanford faculty and investigators, and on-site biotechnology training.
M-TRAM focuses on three integrated areas representing key stages of translational medicine. Rather than specializing in only one area, students develop strong working knowledge across all three domains throughout the program:
Early Translation: Basic Science to Preclinical
Clinical Translation: Clinical Investigation
Late Translation: Medical Adoption and Commercialization
There are two parallel but complementary M-TRAM Tracks based on the type of capstone project students pursue:

individual capstone research project
