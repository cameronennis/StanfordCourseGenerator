Overview
The department requires the completion of
nine courses
(totaling not less than 45 units) of graduate work; seven courses of this work must be Department of History courses. Of the seven, one must be a seminar, and four must be graduate colloquia or graduate seminars. Directed reading may be counted for a maximum of 10 units. A candidate whose undergraduate training in history is deemed inadequate must complete nine courses of graduate work in the department.
Fulfill ALL of the following requirements:
History Courses
Students must take seven courses in the Department of History.
These courses must have the HISTORY subject code.
AND
Graduate Colloquia or Graduate Seminar Courses
Of the seven courses required:
At least one history graduate seminar (400-level)
At least four history graduate colloquia (300-level)
AND
Other Coursework
Students should fulfill the remaining units with coursework that fits the following conditions:
Only courses with a prefix of HISTORY and a number of at least 100
Up to 10 units may be non-HISTORY
