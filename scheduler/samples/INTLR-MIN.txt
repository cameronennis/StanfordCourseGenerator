Program Overview
The International Relations (IR) minor is an interdisciplinary program focusing on how global, regional, and domestic factors influence relations between actors in the modern state system. IR minors may focus on various issues, including international security, international trade and finance, political and economic development, human rights and humanitarian policy, democratization, climate change, global health, and other countries and world regions’ politics, history, and cultures.

Requirements Overview
A minor in International Relations (IR) is intended to provide an interdisciplinary background allowing a deeper understanding of contemporary international issues. To declare the IR minor, students must complete the application for a minor in Axess, complete the IR Minor Declaration and Course Proposal form, and submit this to the IR office.
Students complete the minor by taking 30 units from the IR curriculum that do not duplicate the student’s major (or, if applicable, any other minor).
