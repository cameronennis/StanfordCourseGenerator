Program Overview
The mission of the undergraduate degree in French is to expose students to various perspectives on the French language, culture, and history by providing majors and minors with training in writing and communication as well cultural, textual, and historical analysis. Through such skills, students develop into critical and global thinkers prepared for business, social service, journalism, and government careers or graduate study in French.
Declaring the Minor
To declare, students should complete the
French declaration worksheet

DLCL Undergraduate Student Services Officer
via email, before completing the final step of declaration via Axess.
Requirements Overview
To earn a minor in French, students' plans must adhere to these common overarching requirements:
Complete at least six courses at three units or more and 24 overall units of coursework in the department.
These 24 units may not be used toward any other major or minor.
Courses applied to the minor must be taken for a letter grade, and a grade point average (GPA) of 2.0 or better must be achieved in each course.

Preparing for the Minor
To enroll in all French literature courses taught in French, students must pass first-year French [FRENLANG 1, 2, 3 or FRENLANG 1A, 2A] or complete the placement exam.
Additionally, students are highly encouraged to complete
FRENLANG124
Advanced French: Composition, Writing, and Presentation or successfully tested above this level through the Language Center.
Complete ANY of the following Courses:
FRENLANG124 - Advanced French: Composition, Writing, and Presentation
Required Courses
Complete at least 2 FRENCH courses for a total of at least 8 units.
At least 1 of the required courses must be taken in the French language at the 100- or 200-level (FRENLANG 124 is the only language class that counts for this requirement).
Courses listed below are EXAMPLES and students are not restricted to this list.
Students should refer to course descriptions to
make sure the course is being taught in French the year they take it.
Complete at least 2 of the following Courses:

Electives
Coursework within the department.
Additional FRENCH courses taught by French faculty (may be conducted in English or French) at the 100- or 200- level.
Language coursework.
Up to three language courses in French at or above FRENLANG 21C for a maximum of 12 units.
Coursework in other departments relevant to the degree, with approval by the Director of Undergraduate Studies.
Bing Overseas Study Program.
Courses taken at the Bing Overseas Studies in Paris program with prior approval of the Director of Undergraduate Studies.
Education as Self-Fashioning
(ESF) courses taught at least partially by a French and Italian Department faculty member. Students may count a maximum of ten units.
Structured Liberal Education.
Students may count up to 10 units of SLE toward the major electives.
Digital Humanities course.
Student work must reflect French interests. Prior approval of the Director of Undergraduate Studies. Maximum of five units.
