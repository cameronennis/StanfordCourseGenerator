Program Overview
The minor in Energy Science and Engineering enables a deeper understanding of energy fundamentals and applications to allow students to engage solutions to real-world energy applications and problems. The minor is intended to allow substantial work in Energy Science and Engineering.

Program Requirements
Completion requirement
The minor in Energy Science and Engineering requires the following three courses plus three additional electives. Courses must be planned in consultation with an ESE advisor. Appropriate substitutions are allowed with the consent of the advisor.
Core Course Requirements
Complete ALL of the following Courses:
ENERGY131 - Energy and the Environment
ENERGY132 - Fundamentals of Renewable Energy Processes
ENERGY133 - Economic and Policy Analysis for Sustainable Energy Decisions
Electives
Complete at least 3 of the following Courses:
