Program Overview
Upon declaring the minor, students select an advisor with whom they plan their study and electives. A proposed course of study must be approved by the advisor and placed in the student’s departmental file.
Minors must attend an orientation session presented by the professional staff of the Art & Architecture Library, which introduces the many tools of research and reference available on campus or through the Internet. This requirement should be completed before the quarter following the minor declaration.

Completion requirement
Unnamed Rule
Courses may not be offered every year and are subject to change. Consult the Student Services Specialist if a course is not listed below.
Courses that use film and media to simply illustrate a cultural topic may not be considered. Only those courses that center on film and media and use the methods of film and media study may be used toward completion of the minor.
