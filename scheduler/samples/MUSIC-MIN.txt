Completion requirement
Requirements Overview

Students must complete at least three units of coursework in three of the following five areas:

Composition

History/Ethnomusicology

MST/Computer Music

Performance/Conducting

Theory

Complete at least 3 of the following:
Composition
Earn at least 3 credits from the following:
MUSIC20AY - Exploring Soundscapes: Music-Making as a tool for Mental Wellbeing

MUSIC20C - Jazz Arranging and Composition

MUSIC112 - Film Scoring

MUSIC113 - Introduction to Instrumental Composition

MUSIC124A - Songwriters Workshop

MUSIC125 - First Individual Undergraduate Projects in Composition I

MUSIC127A - Instrumentation and Orchestration

OR
History/Ethnomusicology
Earn at least 3 credits from the following:
MUSIC1A - Music, Mind, and Human Behavior

MUSIC1C - Love Songs

MUSIC2A - The Symphony

MUSIC18A - Introduction to Jazz History and Appreciation

MUSIC40 - Music History to 1600

MUSIC41 - Music History 1600-1830

MUSIC42 - Music History Since 1830

OR
MST/Computer Music
Earn at least 3 credits from the following:
MUSIC101 - Introduction to Creating Electronic Sounds

MUSIC105 - Creative Music Technology Practice

MUSIC128 - Stanford Laptop Orchestra: Composition, Coding, and Performance

MUSIC158 - Musical Acoustics

MUSIC192A - Foundations of Sound-Recording Technology

MUSIC220A - Fundamentals of Computer-Generated Sound

MUSIC320 - Introduction to Audio Signal Processing

OR
Performance/Conducting
Earn at least 3 credits from the following:
MUSIC12A - Introductory Piano Class

MUSIC65A - Voice Class I

MUSIC130B - Elementary Instrumental Conducting

MUSIC160 - Stanford Symphony Orchestra

MUSIC161A - Stanford Wind Symphony

MUSIC171 - Chamber Music

MUSIC172A - Piano

OR
Theory
Earn at least 3 credits from the following:
MUSIC19A - Introduction to Music Theory

MUSIC19B - Intermediate Music Theory

MUSIC21 - Introduction to Tonal Theory

MUSIC22 - Intermediate Tonal Theory

MUSIC23 - Advanced Tonal Theory

MUSIC122A - Counterpoint

MUSIC122B - Analysis of Tonal Music
