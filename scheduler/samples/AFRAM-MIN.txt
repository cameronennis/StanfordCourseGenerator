Program Overview
The Department of African and African American Studies (DAAAS) equips students with the skills to understand how Africa and its diasporas are distinct worlds, yet bound by history and in constant conversation. The DAAAS major takes both African and diasporic worlds seriously, exploring the diversity of African and African-descended experiences while tracing the connections that link them. Our courses examine how African and African-descended peoples helped build the modern world and how they will shape what comes next - from global politics and artificial intelligence to cultural and artistic innovation. Students learn to think rigorously about the evolving meanings of identity, power, freedom, creativity, innovation, and social responsibility in an interconnected world. Graduates of DAAAS pursue careers in law and public policy, the arts, media, business, medicine, education, science and technology, and public service.
