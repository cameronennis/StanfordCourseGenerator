Program Overview

Chemical engineers are responsible for the conception and design of processes for producing, transforming, and transporting materials.

School Requirements
Completion requirement
Mathematics

Complete a minimum of 15 units of Math coursework.

Fulfill ALL of the following requirements:
Calculus
Complete ALL of the following Courses:
MATH19 - Calculus

MATH20 - Calculus

MATH21 - Calculus

AND
Additional Calculus

Students must complete either CME 100, or Math 51 and 52.

Complete at least 1 of the following Courses:
CME100 - Vector Calculus for Engineers

MATH51 - Linear Algebra, Multivariable Calculus, and Modern Applications
AND MATH52 - Integral Calculus of Several Variables

Science

Complete a minimum of 23 units of Science coursework.

Complete ALL of the following Courses:
CHEM31E - Chemical Foundations and 21st Century Problems

CHEM33 - Structure and Reactivity of Carbon-Based Molecules

CHEM121 - Understanding the Natural and Unnatural World through Chemistry

PHYSICS41 - Mechanics
OR PHYSICS41E - Mechanics, Concepts, Calculations, and Context
OR PHYSICS61 - Mechanics and Special Relativity

PHYSICS43 - Electricity and Magnetism
OR PHYSICS81 - Electricity and Magnetism Using Special Relativity and Vector Calculus

Technology in Society (TiS)

Complete one course, at least 3 units or more, from the SoE-approved TiS list found on the Engineering (BS) University Bulletin page.

Engineering Fundamentals
Complete ALL of the following Courses:
CHEMENG20 - Introduction to Chemical Engineering

CHEMENG55 - Foundational Biology for Engineers
