Requirements Overview
Completing a minimum of 35 units in Psychology is required for the minor, including
course
Introduction to Psychology and
course
Introduction to Statistical Methods: Precalculus or a comparable statistics course. Advanced Placement (AP) credit may not be used toward the Psychology minor.
Independent study, research, and practice cannot be counted toward the minor. Summer quarter Psychology courses are not applicable toward the 35 units needed for the minor.
All courses used to fulfill the minor’s requirements must be taken at Stanford (courses taken outside of Stanford cannot be petitioned to count) and passed with a grade of C- or better, except for courses offered only on a satisfactory/no credit basis.

The remaining unit requirements can be fulfilled through elective PSYCH courses.
These can include additional courses from Areas A and B and other electives.
