Program Overview
The minor in Portuguese is for students who want to combine acquisition of linguistic competence with studying the literatures and cultures of the Lusophone world.
Declaring the Minor
To declare, students should complete the
Portuguese declaration worksheet
, sending it to the department Director of Undergraduate Studies (DUS) for approval. Once signed, they should send the worksheet to the
DLCL Undergraduate Student Services Officer
via email, before completing the final step of declaration via Axess.
Requirements Overview
To earn a minor in Portuguese, students' plans must adhere to these common overarching requirements:
Complete at least six courses at three units or more and 23 overall units of coursework in the department.
These 23 units may not be used toward any other major or minor.
Courses applied to the minor must be taken for a letter grade.

Required Courses
Fulfill ALL of the following requirements:
Two ILAC courses with a significant Lusophone component
Students must choose two (2) ILAC courses with a Lusophone component to fulfill this requirement with the approval of the DUS, including courses taken with Stanford ILAC faculty abroad.
AND
Electives
Students should take additional 100- or 200- level courses in literature and culture to meet the minimum unit requirement.
Language coursework.
Up to 12 second-year or above Portuguese language units may count towards the degree, not including conversational, oral communication, business, or medical language courses.
Independent Study.
With the approval of the Director of Undergraduate Studies, Independent Study courses may count towards the degree.
Bing Overseas Program.
Up to 10 units of pre-approved BOSP coursework may contribute to the minor.
Coursework outside the department.
One approved 3-5 unit course (5 units maximum) from outside ILAC or the Language Center.
